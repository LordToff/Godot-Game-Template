# Ash & Oath art

crypt_arena.png and character_portraits.png were generated for this prototype with OpenAI image generation on 26 October 2026.

The portrait atlas is arranged as Vesper / Rook on the first row, Wren / The Hollow on the second. The arena art is decorative; the 9×9 grid and highlighted cells define legal movement. Painted scenery does not block paths.

Original generation files are retained in /workspace/generated_images. Existing font and template licenses remain applicable to those separate assets.
