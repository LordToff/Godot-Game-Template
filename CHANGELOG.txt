# v0.9 — instant rolls, clearer abilities and painted art

- **No rolling animations.** Dice results appear immediately. Rolls remain random; the combat log records them. Attack, Block, buff, debuff and Channel effects remain.
- **Clear ability labels.** Names show category plus special timing: `Contained Power (Skill - Channel)`, `Shield Wall (Defense - Reaction)`, `Strike (Attack)` and `Ashstep (Movement)`. Used in face selection, forge dropdowns, battle dice, Lab and dice details. A face that creates a later reaction is labelled by its own timing.
- **Simpler skill selection.** Larger cards, hero tabs with selection counts, search, category and timing filters. Click a card to inspect its rules in a fixed panel, then Add / Remove. Confirm two faces for each hero. No changes to the 47-face pool or legal builds.
- **More art.** A painted candlelit crypt, three hero portraits and a boss portrait. The board uses thin grid lines instead of heavy boxed tiles. Colored frames identify heroes; legal targets and Channel effects remain visible. The scenery does not change movement rules.
- **Visible Soul option.** Blank → Move 2 ON/OFF above battle dice and in Options. One Blank per hero turn when ON. Toggling does not refill the allowance.

Why: speed up play, make ability timing easier to recognize, reduce selection friction and give the battlefield a cohesive visual style.

No combat balance, ability effects or dice probabilities changed. The old v0.8 guide describes historical roll animations; this note supersedes those instructions.


# v0.8.2 — clearer controls and Blank movement

- **Soul switch during combat:** above the hero dice, with a clear ON/OFF label. Previously it was only in the forge and Lab.
- **Game Options:** top right, during face selection, and below battle controls. Explains exactly what the switch does and includes sound and display settings.
- **ON:** each hero may spend one rolled Blank as Move 2 per own turn. **OFF:** this movement is disabled. Applies immediately and carries into the next encounter. Toggling never refreshes a spent allowance. Build presets still save the choice.
- **Dice cards:** ability names have a separate line; selected dice have a stronger outline. An available Soul conversion is named on the Blank die itself.
- **Battle layout:** separate panels for next-step guidance, boss intent, dice and log. Turn help no longer overlaps instructions. Longer instructions have a hover tooltip.
- **Log:** larger text in the side panel, with full history and filters retained.

Why: the Soul option was hard to find and battle controls competed for attention. No combat balance or roll probabilities changed.

Ash & Oath v0.8.1 · changes and reasons

Test Soul · v0.8.1
Enable the reward Soul before fighting
The forge now has a Test Soul checkbox beside Restore balanced build. ON lets each hero spend one rolled Blank as Move 2 per own turn, without first beating the boss. OFF uses the normal starting Soul.

The Lab has the same toggle, and the board shows the active hero’s remaining allowance. Switching it off and on does not refill a spent conversion. Normal and recovery turns refresh it.

Build presets save this choice. Why: make the reward Soul easy to test without a full victory first.

Dice & clarity · v0.8
Real 3D dice, readable results
Hero and boss dice settle on their recorded ability faces. Numeric rolls and individual rerolls show only the affected dice. Fast rolls and Skip keep results unchanged.

Channel visibility
Persistent rune rings identify Stonewake (blue), Contained Power (purple) and Last Pulse (green). Hover for its effect. Rings fade and pulse when the Channel ends.

More helpful controls
Ready / Spent / Reaction held / Channel held / Resolve next labels, legal-target highlights, specific error messages, friendly-fire confirmation, and a Summary / All-events log toggle.

Practice and presets
Eight guided lessons, a two-face Lab combination setup, ability readiness labels and Save / Load build presets. Why: make each decision visible instead of relying on the full history.

Rule fixes · v0.8
Pierce and Downed
Bypassed Block is no longer restored to a hero who was Downed or killed by the hit.

Break Rhythm
Delays the upcoming boss activation by one slot. The original initiative order returns at the next round.

Short Shot cap ruling
The cap of 1 includes Mark Prey. A damaging hit consumes the mark, but the extra damage cannot take Short Shot above 1.

Channel timing ruling
Contained Power benefits from the shared roll wait for Maintain at the hero’s next turn. Releasing the Channel forfeits them.

Repeated Attacks
Each repetition rechecks its target and rolls fresh Numeric damage. Hooked Slash and Engrave apply their riders to each hit.

Balance constants remain unchanged; these interaction rules can still affect outcomes.

Reliability · v0.7
Downed heroes lose temporary effects
Reviving no longer keeps stale Numeric buffs, marks, wards, Debt or stored reaction modifiers. Physical die mutations remain.

Channels break on a lethal hit too
Contained Power now leaves its Red Tile and Last Pulse becomes Blank even when the triggering hit Downs or kills its hero. Damage prevention does not falsely break a Channel.

No silent Channel replacement
A second Channel cannot overwrite an existing one. The game explains the current one-Channel limit; the new die can be declined.

Clearer documentation
The new short playtest guide separates current rules, ability interpretations and unfinished features. Balance values are unchanged. Why: prevent hidden state changes during ordinary play.

Reward Soul · v0.6
One Blank conversion per hero turn

The first boss reward Soul lets each hero spend at most ONE rolled Action Die Blank as Move 2 during their turn. The installed face stays Blank. Other Blanks may be spent without an effect.

The allowance resets at that hero’s next normal turn or full recovery turn. It is independent for each hero. Failed moves do not spend it. Rerolls and shared rolls do not refill it during the same turn.

Why
Keeps the reward useful without letting multiple Blank rolls replace dedicated movement faces. The starting Soul still gives no Blank movement.

Why

Dice & turn flow
Two Blanks instead of three.
Each die may now hold four abilities: up to 12 total. Default builds add a fourth Strike, a second Step and a second Guard. Ownership and the three-per-category caps still apply.

Why
Empty rolls dominated play. Extra useful faces create more decisions; one saved starting reroll remains. This construction change applies in both encounter modes.

Turn explanations
The chronicle explains turn starts; Explain this turn covers rerolls, available dice, targets, reactions, Block expiry and shared round rolls. View dice distinguishes installed faces from already-recorded results.

New movement
Ashstep
Move up to 2 orthogonal spaces, turning allowed, then gain 1 Block. Occupied cells block the path.

Oathbound Exchange
Swap with a standing ally within 3 and clear sight. Both heroes gain 1 Block. Cannot target the boss, a dead ally or a Downed ally.

Why
Ashstep supports safe repositioning. Exchange rescues an ally or changes the front line. Both help escape Cinder Brand. Choose them from the 47-face extended pool or try them in the Lab.

Boss changes
Ashen Chain
Pull the nearest hero within 3 one legal cell closer and strip 1 Block. No HP damage.

Cinder Brand
Mark a red cross under a hero within 3. At the next boss turn start, heroes still on it take 1 normal damage. Move away to evade. Brands cannot stack.

Grave Tithe
Steal 1 Block from each standing hero within 2, gaining at most 2 Block.

Why
The boss now pressures positioning and defense instead of repeating attacks. Tuned HP is 16 to account for fuller hero dice. Original encounter mode retains the previous boss dice and 18 HP.
