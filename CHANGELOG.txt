# v0.14 — Blank movement, quick face selection and debuff effects

## Blank movement

Blank movement now uses one numeric counter for its remaining allowance. A second legacy “spent” flag can no longer override a refreshed counter. Normal turns and full recovery turns share the same reset function, and the battle log explicitly records each refresh.

The combat controls explain the difference between:

- **This turn’s allowance used:** it refreshes at the next own turn.
- **This particular die spent:** select another ready die; dice roll again next round.
- **Rooted:** voluntary movement is blocked until the next boss turn; a failed attempt does not spend the Blank or its allowance.
- **Soul OFF or movement set to zero:** enable or configure the Soul before using Blank movement.

Trying to move with Soul OFF no longer silently spends a Blank without moving. You can still deliberately resolve or decline an inert Blank. The default remains one Blank → Move 2 per hero turn; balance settings still control movement distance and uses. Toggling Soul OFF/ON never restores spent uses.

The reported once-per-combat lock did not reproduce in the basic unchanged v0.13 rules test. The updated normal-combat UI was then checked across four complete rounds: all three heroes successfully converted a Blank on every own turn, including a test with a stale legacy flag. No boss balance values were changed.

## Pick and place your two faces

The opening screen shows the full bonus pool on the left and all three live unfolded dice on the right.

1. **Click a card once** to select it and equip it into a legal space automatically.
2. **Drag a card onto a die** to auto-fit it on that die, or **onto a square** to choose its exact position.
3. **Drag an installed square onto another square** to swap the two faces.
4. Choose **Next hero** after two designs. When all three heroes are ready, choose **Start battle** directly.

Your placements are preserved when switching heroes and starting combat. The game still enforces two distinct bonus designs, owned copies, body caps and minimum Blanks.

If you drop a new design onto a required Blank, the game can clear one starter square on that same die to preserve the Blank minimum. It tells you which starter was removed. If the requested placement cannot fit the body caps, it is rejected without changing your choices. Dropping a third design onto one of your two selected bonus faces replaces that design when legal. The Remove button remains available, and search / category / timing filters remain.

## Rooted and Weakness on the sprites

- **Rooted:** pulsing roots wrap around the knight’s legs, with visible shackles and a root badge.
- **Weakness:** purple motes drift around the knight, with a broken-sword badge.
- Both effects remain visible on inactive heroes and can appear together. Each disappears when its actual status ends. Hover text and the active-turn Effects summary still explain the rules.

These visuals use no gameplay random numbers and do not change damage, movement, or status durations.

# v0.13 — boss rules and phase upgrades

## Phase one versus phase two

The phase changes **once**, when the boss reaches **half its starting HP or lower**. With the default 12 HP boss, that is 6 HP. The Ability Lab uses 30 HP, so its threshold is 15 HP.

| Ability | Before half HP | At half HP and below |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cinder Brand | Marks the original five-cell **cross**. | New casts mark a **3×3 square**, including corners. |
| Ashen Chain | Pulls and strips Block. | Also applies **Rooted 1** until the boss’s next turn starts. |
| Furnace Roar | Not on the boss die. | Replaces Brace on Die I. The first Roar replaces the next unresolved boss die. |

A Brand that was already armed keeps its original marked cells until it detonates. Crossing half HP does not silently enlarge an existing warning. Custom Brand radius affects the cross arms in phase one and the square radius in phase two.

**Rooted 1:** prevents voluntary walking, Stride, leaping, Ashstep, Blank movement, Exchange and reaction steps. Attacks and defense still work. Forced movement from pushes or pulls still works. Rooted ends at the next **boss** turn start, not at the hero’s turn. Its value and expiry appear in hero hover text and the active-turn status display. This expiry is the requested prototype rule; the source document’s provisional Rooted expiry was different.

## Targeting and attacks

| Ability | What it does now |
| --- | --- |
| Advance | Chooses a standing hero with the **highest current HP** and moves up to 2 toward that hero. Highest-HP ties are random. If already adjacent to **any** hero tied for highest HP, stays and gains **1 Block**. Arriving adjacent after moving does not also grant this Block. If its chosen hero has no legal route, it stays and gains fallback Block. |
| Distort | Targets a highest-current-HP hero if one is within range. If none of the globally highest-HP heroes is reachable, targets the **closest hero within range**. Ties are random. With no hero in range, uses fallback. |
| Drive Back | Pushes automatically along the direction from the boss to the struck hero. Heroes no longer choose a push direction. A collision still causes the existing unavoidable damage and Stunned. If the geometry gives two equally aligned directions, the game chooses randomly. Choosing a die for Stunned is still a player decision. |
| Smoking Strike | Replaces the boss’s plain Strike name. Default attack damage stays **1**. If it causes actual HP loss, puts **one fire tile** beneath the struck hero. If a Smoking Strike fire tile is already there, consumes it and deals **1 extra normal damage** on that cell instead. A fully blocked or reduced hit neither places nor detonates fire. |

Fire stays on its original cell when the hero moves. Another hero can later occupy and trigger that cell. Stepping onto fire alone does not detonate it. These fire tiles are separate from Cinder Brand’s timed warning cells. The extra damage is normal damage, so Block and Damage Reduction apply; it is not unavoidable damage.

**These targeting rules and Smoking Strike apply in both phases.** Only Brand geometry, Chain’s Rooted effect and the availability of Furnace Roar change at half HP. No additional damage upgrades were added.

## What you can see and test

- The boss shows **current / maximum HP and Block beneath its sprite**, in addition to the existing top display.
- At the phase transition, ember rings and sparks flare, a brief “The Hollow ignites” announcement appears, and the boss changes to a separate burning-crown pixel sprite. The header continues to say Phase II after the animation ends.
- Upgraded Cinder Brand and Ashen Chain intents show a **+**. Their descriptions explain the current phase’s effect.
- Smoking Strike fire stays visibly marked on the grid. Hover a burning cell for its detonation rule.
- **Lab tools → Test boss phase II** reduces the Lab boss to its actual threshold and triggers the real transition. Reset test returns to phase one.
- Balance workshop adds Advance’s adjacency Block, phase-II Rooted value and Smoking Strike fire damage. Existing saved Strike damage / range settings still control Smoking Strike. There are now **201** numeric settings. Saved custom profiles are preserved.

## Checks and balance comparison

Targeted rules checks cover phase-gated cross / square geometry, unchanged armed warnings, Rooted movement restrictions and expiry, forced movement, highest-HP pursuit, Distort’s fallback, automatic pushes in all four directions, collisions, and Smoking Strike placement / detonation / prevention. UI checks cover the real Lab phase transition, animation event, separate sprite, status text and disabled rooted reaction steps.

With the existing simple automated strategy and unchanged 12 HP boss, 100 starter-only encounters produced **17 hero wins / 83 boss wins / no stalls**. The existing bonus-pair builds produced **59 / 41 / no stalls**. The new boss is stronger than v0.12, especially against starter-only builds. These scripts do not plan around persistent fire and are a comparison, not a forecast of human results. No compensating HP or damage changes were made in this update; the workshop remains available for experiments.

# v0.12 — changes from the previous browser build

These are the new **Tuned** defaults. Your deliberately saved custom values still override defaults. To try this balance, open **Menu → Game options → Balance workshop → Reset all → Apply**. This restarts the fight. The Soul ON/OFF switch is separate; Blank movement remains Move 2, once per hero turn unless you edit it.

| Change | New behavior | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Strike | 1 damage, previously 2. | Requested reduction in reliable flat damage. |
| Numeric die | The six ordinary sides are **2, 2, 1, 1, 1, 0**. Applies to attacks, healing and defense. The old Tuned +1 attack bonus is now 0. | Let the printed roll provide the strength, without an additional hidden flat bonus. |
| Short Shot | Final damage cap 2, including Mark Prey; previously 1. | Let the new Numeric 2 matter for the ranged attack. |
| Stonewake | Any actual HP loss, or **2 cumulative Block lost before your next own turn**, immediately ends its Channel. Damage, theft and voluntarily burned Block count. Fully prevented damage does not. The counter resets on your next own turn if the Channel survives. | Stop indefinite maintenance behind a shield. Separate one-Block losses add up. |
| Engrave | Only grows after dealing HP damage, at most once per hero turn. Adds a copy only to a Blank on its own die; maximum 3 copies. At 3 copies, +1 damage. Never overwrites another ability. | Stop rapid multiplication, growth from fully blocked hits and replacement of the whole die. |
| Boss durability and Strike | Tuned boss HP **16 → 12**; boss Strike damage **2 → 1**. Original base keeps its 18 HP / 2-damage boss Strike. | Compensate for halving starter Strike damage and adding a boss phase. |
| Cinder Brand | Marks a **3×3 square**, including corners, instead of a five-cell cross. Detonates at next boss turn start for 1 normal damage. Clipped at arena edges; no stacking. | A clearer and more useful area threat, with time to escape. |
| Half-health boss phase | At **50% HP or lower**, once per encounter, unlock **Furnace Roar**. The next unresolved boss die becomes Roar. It also replaces Brace on Die I for future rolls. | A visible change in boss behavior midway through the fight. |
| Furnace Roar | Attack every standing hero within 2 of the boss footprint for 1, then push survivors up to 1 legal cell away. Normal defensive reactions apply. Blocked pushes stop; no collision damage or Stun. | Pressure close formations without adding a large unavoidable damage spike. |

A broken Stonewake die stays spent until the next shared roll; its installed face is preserved. Voluntarily releasing it still rolls the released die immediately. New numeric parameters are available in the balance workshop.

## Reading and editing the dice

- Clicking a combat ability opens a **scrollable description on the far right**, temporarily replacing the log. It never covers the battlefield. Held reactions and Channels can also be inspected. Close it to restore the log. Boss intent buttons use the same panel.
- Hover a hero for their full status list, including values. The active hero also has an Effects summary; full explanations appear in the ability panel and hover text.
- Player HP uses dots again. With a custom maximum above 12, the display adds the exact number beneath the dots.
- Both the forge and View dice use your reference's unfolded net: four squares down the middle, with one square to either side of the second square. All 18 side assignments stay on their original dice. The net is a layout, not a change in roll odds.
- View dice retains rolled-side highlights, changed-face highlights and original-face history.
- Lab tools can force the next Numeric result to 0, 1 **or 2**.

## Rerolls

Blank rerolls are legal during the opening window. A reroll can land on Blank again, including the same side. It still spends the charge and keeps the new result. The log now prints old and new side numbers and face names, and explicitly explains repeated results.

The reroll button now uses the same eligibility check as the rules and explains why it is disabled. It cannot silently offer a spent die or bypass a result that Focus or another ability requires you to resolve next. The opening-window rule remains: spend ordinary Action Rerolls before your first normal action. Focus can still reroll another unresolved die later.

## Verification

100 seeded automated encounters with starter faces only: **47 hero wins / 53 boss wins / 0 stalls**. With the existing bonus-pair automated builds: **72 / 28 / 0 stalls**. Before reducing boss HP and Strike, the same new hero rules gave only 1 starter-build win in 100 against the old boss stats. These scripts use simple fixed strategies; the results are a comparison, not predicted human win rates.

Targeted checks cover cumulative Stonewake interruption, prevented/HP/stripped damage, own-turn resets, actual Numeric rolls and all six values, Engrave limits, square fire corners, the once-only half-HP transition, Roar reactions and pushing, and repeated-Blank rerolls. UI checks verify the nets, full status descriptions and a side panel that leaves the board unobstructed.

Reward Soul · v0.6
One Blank conversion per hero turn

The first boss reward Soul lets each hero spend at most ONE rolled Action Die Blank as Move 2 during their turn. The installed face stays Blank. Other Blanks may be spent without an effect.

The allowance resets at that hero’s next normal turn or full recovery turn. It is independent for each hero. Failed moves do not spend it. Rerolls and shared rolls do not refill it during the same turn.

Why
Keeps the reward useful without letting multiple Blank rolls replace dedicated movement faces. The starting Soul still gives no Blank movement.

Ashstep
Move up to 2 orthogonal spaces, turning allowed, then gain 1 Block. Occupied cells block the path.

Oathbound Exchange
Swap with a standing ally within 3 and clear sight. Both heroes gain 1 Block. Cannot target the boss, a dead ally or a Downed ally.

Why
Ashstep supports safe repositioning. Exchange rescues an ally or changes the front line. Both help escape Cinder Brand. Choose them from the 47-face extended pool or try them in the Lab.

Steal 1 Block from each standing hero within 2, gaining at most 2 Block.
