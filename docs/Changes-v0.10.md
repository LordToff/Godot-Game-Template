# v0.10 — pixel art and a larger battlefield

- **Replaced the painted art.** New pixel sprites, small head icons and simple stone tiles use a limited palette and sharp scaling. The previous painted assets have been removed from the game. The pixel maps and generator are included in the source.
- **Enlarged the arena.** It is now 684×684 screen pixels, up from 495×495 — about 91% more screen area. It still has nine rows and nine columns, so movement and balance rules have not changed.
- **Simplified the battle UI.** A compact toolbar, one turn banner, three boss-intent buttons, grouped dice and turn controls, and small party cards below the arena. Removed repeated instructions and abbreviated party dice lists.
- **Kept the log on the side.** It remains tall and scrollable, with full history and filters available.
- **Moved secondary tools.** Open Menu for practice, rules, changes, options or rebuilding. Open Lab tools for ability selection, forced Numeric values, test attacks, combinations and resets. Boss-intent buttons still explain every action.

The Soul ON/OFF switch, once-per-turn limit, timing labels, instant rolls, attack / Block / buff / debuff effects and Channel indicator remain. No ability effects, dice probabilities or combat balance changed.

Why: make the board the focus, reduce clutter, and give the prototype a consistent pixel-art style.
