# v0.8.2 — clearer controls and Blank movement

- **Soul switch during combat:** above the hero dice, with a clear ON/OFF label. Previously it was only in the forge and Lab.
- **Game Options:** top right, during face selection, and below battle controls. Explains exactly what the switch does and includes sound and display settings.
- **ON:** each hero may spend one rolled Blank as Move 2 per own turn. **OFF:** this movement is disabled. Applies immediately and carries into the next encounter. Toggling never refreshes a spent allowance. Build presets still save the choice.
- **Dice cards:** ability names have a separate line; selected dice have a stronger outline. An available Soul conversion is named on the Blank die itself.
- **Battle layout:** separate panels for next-step guidance, boss intent, dice and log. Turn help no longer overlaps instructions. Longer instructions have a hover tooltip.
- **Log:** larger text in the side panel, with full history and filters retained.

Why: the Soul option was hard to find and battle controls competed for attention. No combat balance or roll probabilities changed.
