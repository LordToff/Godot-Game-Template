First: what were “Numeric attacks”?

These are attacks that roll a separate die to determine damage. That die is called the Numeric die. Its six sides are 2, 2, 1, 1, 1, 0: 33.3% base 2, 50% base 1 and 16.7% base 0. Modifiers can change the final value. For example, Strike Numeric rolling 2 starts with 2 damage before Block and modifiers. Basic Strike instead has a fixed base damage of 1.

The team is now labelled More attacks with rolled damage. Each hero keeps four Basic Strike faces and equips two more attack faces, replacing one Step and one Focus. It is a choice of bonus abilities, not a character class or a different Numeric die. See for each hero’s two abilities and what they do.

What needs attention?

Blank movement has a large difficulty effect. Random bonus teams won 49.5% with it OFF, 83.0% at Move 1 and 89.0% at Move 2. It should be treated as a substantial optional advantage.

The attack-heavy kit wins more often than the support kit under this bot. With movement OFF: 79% versus 40%. Whole-team comparisons cannot tell us which single ability needs a buff; the support strategy is also less optimized.

No default HP or damage values were changed. The random-bonus / movement-OFF sample does not support a blanket boss HP reduction. Edge-case bugs were fixed; the exact win/loss counts stayed unchanged after those fixes.

These are fixed-strategy bot comparisons, not predicted human win rates. The before/after runs reuse the same 200 seeds per scenario. Read Method & fixes before drawing conclusions about one ability.

Ability and battle language

Action die
Chooses which ability you get to use. Each hero has three six-sided Action dice.
Numeric die
A separate value die used by some attacks, healing and defenses. Its sides are 2, 2, 1, 1, 1, 0. It is not an extra Action die.
Attack with rolled damage
An ability that rolls the Numeric die to set its base damage. For example: Strike Numeric. Modifiers, Block and Weakness can change the final HP damage.
Basic Strike
The ordinary starting attack has base damage 1. It does not roll Numeric for its damage.
Blank movement OFF
A rolled Action-die Blank cannot move a hero. This is the normal starting setting.
Blank → Move 1 or 2
With the test Soul ON, spend one rolled Action-die Blank to move up to 1 or 2 orthogonal spaces. Maximum one conversion per hero’s own turn, including a full recovery turn. Other heroes have their own allowance.
Numeric zero versus Blank
A Numeric result of 0 is a value roll. It cannot be converted into movement. Only a rolled Blank on an Action die can use the test Soul.
Turn versus round
A turn is one hero or the boss acting. A round contains the actors’ turns in initiative order. “Average rounds” is not a count of clicks, dice or individual turns.
Channel
An effect maintained between turns. Its Action die is held rather than rolled. Maintain or release it at the hero’s next turn; its printed interruption rules still apply.
Block and protection
Block absorbs ordinary damage and is consumed. Remaining Block normally expires at that unit’s next turn start. Stonewake can preserve it while maintained.
Downed versus dead
A hero at 0 HP can be Downed and potentially revived. “Downings” counts events, including repeat events; it is not a count of permanently dead heroes. A party with no standing hero loses.
Starting reroll
Each hero starts with one Action Reroll. That charge does not refill every round. Use it in the opening window before ordinary actions.
Test seed
A reproducible random starting seed. Seeds 1–200 were reused across settings and before/after fixes. Changing actions changes later random draws.

What the starting abilities do

Blank
A rolled Action-die Blank has no ordinary effect. With the test Soul ON, one Blank per hero turn may be spent for movement. It stays installed as Blank.
Strike
Basic Strike: attack an adjacent unit for base damage 1. It does not roll the Numeric die. Block and Weakness can reduce the HP damage.
Step
Step: move up to 1 orthogonal space to a legal empty cell. Cannot move while Rooted.
Stride
Stride: move up to 3 orthogonal spaces in one straight line. Cannot turn, pass through units or move while Rooted.
Guard
Guard: gain 1 Block.
Reaction
Stored automatically when rolled. When personally attacked, react to gain 1 Block and optionally step up to 1 space. Rooted prevents the step.
Focus
Reroll another unresolved Action die and resolve its new result next. This spends Focus; it does not use the separate banked Action Reroll charge.
First Aid
Heal yourself or an adjacent hero for 1 HP. Healing a Downed hero revives them as Broken and queues an immediate recovery turn after the acting turn ends.
Shove
Shove an adjacent unit up to 1 legal space away, with no collision damage. This owned starter ability is not equipped in the default trial layouts.

Method and limits

What was held constant

Tuned rules; heroes start at 6 HP, boss at 12 HP. Half HP (6) unlocks phase II. All three Action dice have six sides; minimum two Blanks per die initially. Ordinary Numeric values are 2, 2, 1, 1, 1, 0. Each hero starts with one banked Action Reroll. The latest boss rules and half-HP upgrades are active.

Boss phases in these trials

The boss starts at 12 HP and enters phase II at 6 HP. Cinder Brand starts as a five-cell cross and upgrades to a 3×3 square for newly armed Brands. Upgraded Ashen Chain adds Rooted until the next boss turn. Furnace Roar unlocks at half HP. Smoking Strike places fire after HP damage and detonates existing fire on another damaging hit.

What varied

Six team builds and three Blank movement settings. For the fixed teams, only the selected bonus designs change. The legal auto-builder may replace different starter faces to satisfy body caps; see Dice layouts. Random pairs use all 47 extended designs. All default ability numbers remain unchanged.

How the bot plays

It reads visible rolls and board state, prioritizes healing and revivals, uses setup effects, attacks legal targets and approaches the boss. It tries to leave marked Brand cells, avoids fire / board edges when equivalent, rerolls unusable Blanks in its opening window and keeps Channels. It does not inspect future RNG outcomes.

Before versus after

Seeds 1–200 were used for every scenario before and after the fixes. That is 3,600 fights per version, 7,200 runs total. Every scenario’s win/loss count stayed unchanged. Dedicated regression cases reproduce the fixed edge cases; unchanged aggregate wins do not mean the bugs were harmless.

Limits of the evidence

This is one heuristic bot, not a human playtest or an optimal strategy search. It does not optimize every rescue, tanking or support combination. Reusing the same starting seeds does not force identical later die rolls when decisions change. Whole-team comparisons cannot isolate the balance of one ability.

What “no stalls” means

Each fight had a limit of 1,600 decisions or 50 rounds. No fight reached the limit. HP / Block / dead-state checks found no invalid values. This does not prove that every possible game bug has been tested.

Why the old starter result differed

The earlier simple bot recorded 17 wins in 100 fights. This newer heuristic recorded 67 in 200 with basic dice and movement OFF. Its healing / revival and hazard-escape choices differ. Compare before/after only when the same policy is used.

Balance decision

No default HP, damage or Soul numbers were changed. Random bonus builds with movement OFF won 99/200, which does not support a blanket boss HP reduction. Move 2 plus the attack-heavy kit won 199/200; that optional combination makes this encounter especially forgiving.
